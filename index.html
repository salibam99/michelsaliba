About Me

Nuclear engineer bridging simulation and experiment

I am a PhD candidate in Nuclear Engineering at École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL), working on the design, construction, and commissioning of a new neutron imaging station at the CROCUS zero-power reactor. My research spans Monte Carlo particle transport simulation, neutron noise analysis, gamma spectroscopy, and nuclear reactor kinetics.

Key Projects

Research highlights from my PhD and academic career

Neutron Imaging

CROCUS Neutron Imaging Station

Designed, built, and initiated a new high-precision neutron imaging station at the CROCUS zero-power reactor. Conducted first neutron imaging experiments, expanding the facility's experimental capabilities for nuclear research.

CROCUS Reactor Neutron Radiography EPFL
Monte Carlo Development

OpenMC Event Tracking Feature

Engineered and validated a new event-tracking (collision tracking) feature within the OpenMC simulation code, optimized for neutron spectroscopy, scatter camera systems, neutron noise analysis, and multiplicity counting.

OpenMC C++ Neutron Transport
Reactor Kinetics

SAFFRON 3D Detector System

Developed and deployed SAFFRON — a novel 150-node 3D detector system for precise measurement of the prompt neutron decay constant in a zero-power reactor. Extracted kinetics parameters using neutron noise techniques with Fourier transform algorithms.

SAFFRON Neutron Noise 3D Detector Array
Nuclear Security

Xenon-135 Poisoning Observation

Pioneered advanced gamma-ray spectroscopy methodologies aimed at directly observing and quantifying Xenon-135 poisoning dynamics in real-time in a zero-power reactor, with applications to nuclear safeguards.

Gamma Spectroscopy Xe-135 Nuclear Safeguards
Simulation Engineering

Fuel Assembly Decay Heat Simulation

At Kernkraftwerk Gösgen: Built end-to-end simulation of irradiation cycles of fuel assemblies to compute decay heat using C#. Code was verified against other codes and concluded successfully.

C# Decay Heat KKG
Radiation Protection

LINAC Room Shielding Application

Designed a user-friendly C# WPF application to compute wall thicknesses around LINAC rooms for radiation protection. Fully designed and implemented during internship at Mount-Lebanon Hospital.

C# WPF Radiation Protection
